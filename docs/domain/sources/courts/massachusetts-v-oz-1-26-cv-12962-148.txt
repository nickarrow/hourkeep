COMMONWEALTH OF MASSACHUSETTS, et al.

MEHMET OZ, M.D., in his official capacity as

ORDER OF VACATUR AND ENJOINMENT
October 29, 2026
STEARNS, D.J.
Consistent with the court’s October 29, 2026, Order allowing in part
and denying in part plaintiffs’ motion for summary judgment, it is hereby
ORDERED that:
a) The portion of the Interim Final Rule (IFR) entitled “Community
Engagement Requirement for Certain Individuals,” 91 Fed. Reg.
33348 (June 3, 2026), which defines “medically frail” or “special
medical needs” to include “whose physical, mental, or other
behavioral health condition significantly impairs the individual’s
ability to comply with the community engagement requirement
in this subpart and is an individual” is DECLARED unlawful, and
VACATED and SET ASIDE pursuant to 5 U.S.C. § 706(2).
