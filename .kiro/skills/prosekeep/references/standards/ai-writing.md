# Review AI-writing habits without flattening an author

Sources and inspirations: Vloora's documented editorial corrections, [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer), and [conorbronsdon/avoid-ai-writing](https://github.com/conorbronsdon/avoid-ai-writing). These are distinct upstream tools; Prosekeep does not bundle or claim their complete instruction sets. Review source licenses before copying their text into another distribution.

## Structural patterns to inspect

- A negative contrast creates an imaginary opponent instead of making a claim.
- An introduction announces that an issue is important rather than saying what happened.
- The final sentence summarizes the preceding paragraph without adding information.
- Lists repeatedly appear in groups of three even when there are only one or two distinct claims.
- Identical sentence openings or fragments create artificial cadence across a page.
- A fashionable metaphor hides the actor, mechanism or consequence.
- A technical noun is replaced with a slogan, or the writer is forced into generic corporate language.
- Authority is borrowed from unnamed experts or an irrelevant list of publications.
- Dates, numbers, citations or qualifications vanish during a style rewrite.
- Every item gets the same bold label or decorative heading shape.
- Overuse of dashes, excessive qualifiers and formulaic transitions displaces natural paragraph logic.

## Review rather than ban

A single dash, contrast, rhetorical question, list of three, or long sentence can be intentional. Check against the writer's samples and stated preferences. Prosekeep's line-based checker detects a limited subset, not an AI detector. Never call prose "AI-generated" on a pattern match alone.

## Revision method

Read the whole piece first. Note patterns with precise locations. Rewrite paragraphs around the point they make, preserve distinct voice and supported content, and then compare the revision against the source. Leave expressive irregularity intact when it serves the author's writing.
