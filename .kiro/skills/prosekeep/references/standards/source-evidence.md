# Sources, factual fidelity and evidence

This is Prosekeep's editorial method, informed by the [18F Content Guide](https://guides.18f.org/content-guide/) and the source-preservation requirements developed in Vloora's own review workflow.

## Evidence states

Keep separate: a proposal; an attempted action; an acknowledgment; a party's report of completion; independently verified completion. An assistant's assertion, fixture output, or UI mockup does not establish a real-world outcome.

## Preserve the author's record

Retain names, dates, metrics, quotations, primary sources, URLs, citations, meaningful distinctions, chronology, qualifications, and first-person observations unless authorized to change them. Do not fabricate references, reviews, customers, integrations, tests or adoption. A source cannot support a broader claim than it states.

When editing a scientific or technical paper, preserve methods, scope, limitations and all material uncertainty. For a case study, distinguish what the tool did from what a person observed. For research notes, cite primary records where available and state what the evidence actually measures.

## Before-and-after audit

1. Identify original claims and what supports each.
2. Compare revised claims to original text and cited sources.
3. Flag added, dropped or changed quantitative statements and URLs for a responsible reviewer.
4. Review every apparent contradiction and any new unqualified assertion.
5. Record what could not be verified and who must approve it.

The Prosekeep source-fidelity command can flag differences in URLs, numbers and numbered citations. It does **not** verify quotations, propositions, sources or truth; those require an agent or a person with access to the material.
